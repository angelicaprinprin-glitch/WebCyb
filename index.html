What is phishing?

Phishing is when criminals send messages that pretend to come from someone you trust, like your bank, a delivery company, your school or a popular website. They want you to click a dangerous link, open a harmful file, or give away private information such as passwords, codes or card numbers.

Watch out for emails that rush or scare you, ask for personal information, promise prizes, or come from addresses and links that look almost right, but aren't.

WebCyb checks the email you received for the warning signs of phishing and tells you if it is safe. Copy the whole email, including the sender and subject, and paste it below. Your email never leaves your device: WebCyb checks it right in your browser and doesn't save it.
